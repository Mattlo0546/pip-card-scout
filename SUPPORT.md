# Support

## Installation and usage help

Read the [installation instructions](./README.md#install-in-google-chrome) and [current limitations](./README.md#current-limitations) first. For a reproducible non-sensitive bug or feature request, [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/Mattlo0546/pip-card-scout/issues/new) and include:

- the Pip version from `chrome://extensions`;
- the Google Chrome version and operating system;
- the merchant domain, without a checkout path or order details;
- what you expected and what happened; and
- console output with email addresses, tokens, totals, wallet labels, and personal data removed.

Never post a full card number, CVC/CVV, expiry date, password, authentication token, email address, or another person's information in an issue. Pip support will never need payment credentials.

## Account deletion and private privacy requests

The extension's **Delete account** action permanently removes the signed-in account, wallet, and saved recommendation history. The beta does not yet provide data export. For an access or export request, or help if deletion fails, use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Mattlo0546/pip-card-scout/security/advisories/new), title the report **Privacy request**, and state the Pip account email involved. Do not add wallet labels, checkout history, or payment details unless the maintainer specifically requests the minimum information needed.

## Security reports

Suspected vulnerabilities must follow [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md), not the public issue tracker.

## Card and rewards questions

Pip estimates rewards from published issuer terms. Merchant classification, eligibility, exclusions, caps, and redemption value can change. The issuer's current terms and settled statement are authoritative; Pip cannot resolve account-specific disputes or provide financial advice.
